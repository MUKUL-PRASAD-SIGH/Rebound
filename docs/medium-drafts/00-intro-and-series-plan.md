## Intro draft (reuse for Article 1)

Hey, I'm Mukul.

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering student at M.S. Ramaiah Institute of Technology, Bengaluru, and I spend a slightly unhealthy amount of time building things — especially around AI, developer tools, and real-world problems.

I've participated in quite a few hackathons, built projects that went far beyond the initial idea, broken things that absolutely shouldn't have broken, and learned that the most interesting part of building something isn't the final demo.

It's everything that happens before it.

So, for the Razorpay Buildathon, I decided to document the entire journey — not just the final product.

This is going to be a record of how the idea evolved, what I researched, what I built, what failed, the decisions I made along the way, and how all of that eventually turned into the final product.
