I am deliberately **not** jumping into coding on Day 0.

## The question I keep asking

For every idea:

1. What does Razorpay already do?
2. What is Razorpay actively building in 2026?
3. What does the hackathon ask builders to explore?
4. What do merchants still need?
5. What could *this* project add without recreating an existing feature?

If I cannot answer (1) and (2), I do not get to propose (5).
